Engine Features

Designed Around Your Personal Baseline

Unlike generic wearables that compare you to static population averages, Heart Guard V1 learns your unique body physiology.

🎯

Personal Baseline Relative Metrics

Measures heart rate anomalies in standard deviation Z-scores relative to your established resting baseline, eliminating false alarms from high or low natural resting rates.

🏃

Activity-Gated Alert Engine

Integrated accelerometer state classification automatically relaxes HR thresholds during light activity or exercise, ensuring workout heart rates are never misclassified as anomalies.

🛡️

Multi-Tier Escalation Safeguard

Requires a minimum 3 to 7 consecutive anomalous windows before escalating alert tiers (Normal → Worth Noting → Worth Watching → Medical Attention → SOS). Single noisy readings are discarded.
